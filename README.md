A single 1024×1024 picture that sums up the world's news of Wednesday
25 November 2026. The front page carries 45 sourced stories from 7 regions —
Middle East, Europe, North America, South America, Africa, Asia and Oceania —
drawn by `build_image.py` (Python + Pillow, written from scratch, fully
deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical output).

The hero story is the Strait of Hormuz scare: Iran says tankers hit naval
mines and caught fire, the US military disputes the account, and oil prices
climb. Around it: Shenzhou-24 carries Pakistan's first astronaut to China's
space station, Huawei is convicted in a US court, Spain heads into a snap
election, Australia signs a Pacific treaty with Solomon Islands, and 40 more.

`main.ts` (Node, standard library only) checks the PNG signature of
`entry.png` and copies it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` for the judge.
